# Practical reading guidance

Today, Full, natal interpretation, chart addenda and Zeus share the reader-facing
guidance in `server/supportive-guidance.mjs`. Calculations remain the evidence
for interpretation. User-facing prose gives more space to everyday meaning,
manageable practices, their practical rationale and examples.

Each reading section develops one main suggestion and offers a smaller version
when time or capacity is limited. Technical placement references stay brief;
degrees, orbs and classifications remain available in calculated facts and
requested factual explanations. No instruction treats astrological symbolism
as a proven physiological effect or guarantees an outcome.

Today targets 400–600 words, with four domains and two planetary highlights.
Full keeps four dated horizons and allows 75–110 words per domain. Chart
addenda allow 80–110 words per section and a fuller synthesis of priorities.
Existing schemas, model token caps and the separation between computed facts
and interpretive text are retained.

Narration prioritises practical takeaways when condensing Full content.
Standalone Today narration allows 750 words so expanded guidance is retained;
chart-inclusive and Full narration keep the 1,000-word budget. Existing
character and ten-minute audio limits still apply.

Zeus starts with a useful response to the question, then explains one manageable
practice and its rationale. He can connect it to brief verified astrological
context. His conversational turns remain digestible, under 120 words, with
follow-up available for additional support.
