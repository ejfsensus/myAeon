# Phase 1 reading update

Implemented 15 October 2026 from `reading-expansion-plan.md`.

- Standard Today and Full requests use available server-verified natal facts alongside dated sky and lunar data in a single generation. A standalone chart report remains optional.
- A bounded synthesis ledger records source, settings, dates, input confidence and candidate evidence by domain/horizon. Returned section references are filtered to their allowed evidence. This validates references, not the truth of every sentence of generated interpretation.
- Shared guidance selects a few useful priorities, translates lunar symbolism into optional reflection and adapts suggestions to optional focus/capacity choices. The ordinary four domains and narration budgets remain in place.
- Capacity choices request a style of support; they are not observed symptoms. The shared prompt makes this distinction explicit. Standard reading normalization rejects specific unsupported bodily assertions and dietary restrictions, replacing affected sections with labelled general reflections.
- The Sun foundation prefers the verified natal Sun. Without natal calculation, a birth-day interval in the birthplace timezone preserves sign-boundary ambiguity. Estimated natal positions remain approximate. PDF and Zeus retain the accepted reading's basis and context.
- Search results display region/country names but serialize only the strict birthplace fields. Search supplies coordinates and timezone; manual details are expandable. Reading submission already confirms consented details, so separate Zeus confirmation is secondary.
- The opening wordmark fade is restored on navy, with a reduced-motion alternative and a bounded exit. The welcome screen appears after the transition.

The calculation foundation remains tropical/geocentric. There are no new traditions, exact event searches, inferred transit durations, physical energy scores or permanent history in this release. Additional work belongs to later phases.

Validation includes international search selection and strict profile validation; DST/timezone and Sun-boundary cases; evidence date/domain limits; artifact, PDF, audio and contextual-agent regression tests; TypeScript/production build; and browser checks of the opening and request flow. The optional tests retain their existing prerequisites. Live reading verification uses synthetic birth details.
